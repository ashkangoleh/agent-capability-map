# Three first-class technical domains

```text
Master Capability Router
├── Data Engineering: ingestion, CDC, streaming, storage, compute, orchestration, lakehouse, warehouses, DataOps, reliability and serving
├── Data Science: scientific computing, EDA, statistics, Bayesian methods, experimentation, causal inference, classical ML, forecasting, survival, recommendation, graph, geospatial, optimization
├── AI & ML Engineering: deep learning, transformers, LLM, RAG, vision, speech, distributed training, MLOps and model serving
└── Infrastructure Engineering: DevOps, DevSecOps, SRE, platform engineering, Kubernetes, cloud, networking, systems, storage, security and DR
```

Data Engineering builds trusted data movement, storage, transformation and serving. Data Science formulates quantitative questions, designs experiments and evaluates statistical/classical predictive models. AI/ML Engineering productionizes deep-learning and AI systems. Infrastructure provisions and operates compute, network, platform, identity, security and reliability substrates.

Cross-domain tasks must use typed relations or clarify the primary deliverable. Do not load every related skill by default. This is broad coverage of major ecosystems, not a claim to enumerate every product or research method.
