## Source edition discipline and important restrictions
- `BPMN 2.0.2` is the published formal OMG BPMN specification and does not by itself certify executable workflows: https://www.omg.org/spec/BPMN/2.0.2/
- `DMN 1.6` is the published formal September 2026 specification; `1.7` is beta and not the same assurance baseline: https://www.omg.org/spec/DMN/1.6/
- `ISO 9001:2026` replaces withdrawn `ISO 9001:2015`; check release and applicability before claims about quality systems: https://committee.iso.org/standard/9001
- `ISO 31000:2018` remains risk-management guidance and is not certifiable: https://www.iso.org/standard/31000
- `ISO 22301:2019` with the 2024 amendment is published, while its third edition remains a draft as of 2026-11-06: https://www.iso.org/standard/75106.html
- `IFRS 18` replaces IAS 1 with effective annual periods from 2027-01-01, earlier adoption permitted; management forecasting is not a substitute for audited IFRS financial statements: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/
- `IAS 7` distinguishes operating, investing and financing cash flows: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-7-statement-of-cash-flows.html
- Supplier process advice uses `ISO 20400:2017` procurement guidance, rather than assuming certification: https://www.iso.org/standard/63026.html
- APQC Process Classification Framework is a taxonomy and benchmarking language, not a universal process implementation standard: https://www.apqc.org/process-frameworks
- Organizational risk and oversight source: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/g20-oecd-principles-of-corporate-governance-2023_ed750b30-en.html

## Domain ownership
Business owns decision economics, supplier contracts as evidence, operational process models, risk acceptance and organizational execution. Data Engineering implements pipelines; Data Science handles rigorous model inference; Marketing owns paid advertising; Security owns technical controls; Legal and Finance approve formal legal and accounting decisions. No business role may sign or purchase.
