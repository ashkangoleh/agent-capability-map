# Product Domain Router

Use for product strategy, discovery, requirements, prioritization, roadmaps, analytics, experimentation, and pricing.

1. Read domains/product/skills.json only.
2. Problem framing, discovery, PRDs, user stories, acceptance criteria, and prioritization route to product-discovery-prd.
3. Product analytics or experimentation requires metric definitions, population, denominator, guardrails, and causal limitations.
4. Do not invent customer research, market size, user quotes, or stakeholder decisions.
5. Label assumptions and hypotheses; convert unknowns into research questions or validation plans.
