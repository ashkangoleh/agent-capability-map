# Marketing Domain Router

Use for positioning, go-to-market, brand, campaign planning, paid media, lifecycle, CRM, content, social, and attribution.

1. Read domains/marketing/skills.json only.
2. Campaign or channel strategy routes to marketing-strategy-campaign.
3. Search visibility, crawlability, indexing, SERP, and organic search diagnostics route to the SEO domain.
4. Content production uses this skill only when the task includes a marketing objective, audience, channel, and measurement plan.
5. Never invent audience research, campaign results, conversion rates, budgets, or competitor data.
6. Treat platform policies, ad formats, and prices as time-sensitive and verify current official sources.
