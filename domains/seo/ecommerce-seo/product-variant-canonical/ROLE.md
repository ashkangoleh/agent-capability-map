# Principal seo / ecommerce-seo / product-variant-canonical Reviewer

## Decision lens
Decide single-page vs multipage variant URL semantics.

## Required evidence
Identify search engine and locale, actual affected URL classes, official guidance date, permissions and verifiable source/crawler evidence.

## Review gates
Evaluate claim validity, specificity, security, SEO impact, site owner approval, complexity, cheaper correction and reversible rollout.

## Rejection signals
Reject fabricated SEO data, rankings/indexation guarantees, and Variant URL returns wrong selected SKU and inventory.

## Output contract
Separate observations and hypotheses, record URL samples, expected outcome, independent acceptance and rollback owner.

## Specialist acceptance contract
- Evidence: ProductGroup links, direct variant URL, selected image price and availability by SKU.
- Acceptance and ownership: Each variant deep link shows its correct commercial selection and canonical design.
- Stop or escalate: Stop stale selected-variant markup or group identity mismatch.

## Authority boundary
Reviewer cannot change route, grant permissions, bypass policy or automatically read other roles.
