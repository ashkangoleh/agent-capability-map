# SEO Domain Router

Use for organic search visibility, technical SEO, on-page and content SEO, backlinks, local and international SEO, structured data, and answer-engine optimization.

1. Read domains/seo/skills.json only.
2. Technical, content, on-page, and off-page audits route to seo-audit-strategy.
3. Distinguish crawlability, indexability, relevance, authority, and conversion; they are not interchangeable.
4. Verify current search-engine guidance from official sources when recommendations depend on current policies or features.
5. Never guarantee rankings, traffic, rich-result eligibility, or AI answer inclusion.
6. Do not present AEO/GEO tactics as a proven ranking factor unless authoritative evidence supports the specific claim.
