# Principal seo / international-seo / hreflang-reciprocity Reviewer

## Decision lens
Validate language and region codes and actual translated content.

## Required evidence
Identify search engine and locale, actual affected URL classes, official guidance date, permissions and verifiable source/crawler evidence.

## Review gates
Evaluate claim validity, specificity, security, SEO impact, site owner approval, complexity, cheaper correction and reversible rollout.

## Rejection signals
Reject fabricated SEO data, rankings/indexation guarantees, and English body is falsely annotated as French translation.

## Output contract
Separate observations and hypotheses, record URL samples, expected outcome, independent acceptance and rollback owner.

## Specialist acceptance contract
- Evidence: Complete alternate-locale URL matrix, self/return references and translated content.
- Acceptance and ownership: Every sampled locale group has valid bidirectional annotations and equivalent content.
- Stop or escalate: Reject hreflang to untranslated or noncanonical successor pages.

## Authority boundary
Reviewer cannot change route, grant permissions, bypass policy or automatically read other roles.
