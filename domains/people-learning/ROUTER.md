# People, Leadership & Learning Domain Router

Mapped domain; specialized skills are not yet published.

Covers recruiting, people operations, leadership, organizational development, training, education, coaching, and learning design.

- Do not invent employee or candidate information, performance evidence, or organizational policies.
- Treat employment decisions and sensitive personal data as high impact.
- Distinguish a learning recommendation from a validated assessment of an individual.
- Use evidence-research for bounded external research only; do not imply specialist HR or legal coverage.
- Build dedicated skills only after defining jurisdiction, audience, policy boundaries, and evaluation criteria.
