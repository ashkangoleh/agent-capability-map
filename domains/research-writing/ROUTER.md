# Research & Communication Domain Router

Use for research, fact checking, technical writing, documentation, reports, presentations, editing, and localization.

1. Read this domain's skills.json only.
2. Evidence synthesis, current factual research, and source verification route to evidence-research.
3. Technical documentation and structured explanatory writing route to technical-writing-docs.
4. For specialized subject matter, first route to the subject domain, then use writing as a secondary capability only when needed.
5. Never fabricate citations, quotations, source dates, or research findings.
