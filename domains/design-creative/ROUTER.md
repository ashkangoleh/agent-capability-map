# Design & Creative Domain Router

Use for UX research, interaction design, visual design, design systems, accessibility, brand identity, and creative production.

1. Read this domain's skills.json only.
2. Usability research, UX critique, accessibility review, and interaction design route to ux-research-design-review.
3. Brand positioning and campaign messaging route to marketing unless the deliverable is a visual identity system.
4. Do not invent user-test findings or claim accessibility conformance without testing.
5. Separate heuristic critique from measured usability evidence.
