# Governance & Risk Domain Router

Mapped domain; specialized skills are not yet published.

Covers privacy, compliance, legal information, financial analysis, audit, risk management, and security governance.

- Do not route high-impact advice to a generic skill as if it were specialist advice.
- Use evidence-research only for bounded factual research, not as a substitute for legal, medical, financial, or security professional judgment.
- Verify current law, regulation, and official guidance from authoritative primary sources.
- State jurisdiction, date, scope, and missing facts when relevant.
- If the request has material legal, financial, safety, or privacy consequences, require qualified review.
- This domain is mapped, not capability-complete.
