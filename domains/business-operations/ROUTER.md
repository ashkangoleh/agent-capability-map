# Business & Operations Domain Router

Use for business strategy, operating models, process improvement, business analysis, sales, customer success, partnerships, and operational planning.

1. Read this domain's skills.json only.
2. Strategic options, operating-model analysis, and business-case development route to business-strategy-analysis.
3. Financial projections must expose assumptions, formulas, sensitivities, and uncertainty; do not present estimates as actuals.
4. Do not invent company performance, customer evidence, market size, or stakeholder commitments.
5. Legal, tax, investment, and regulated financial advice routes to governance-risk safeguards.

## Business analytics boundary

Management scorecards, KPI dictionaries, metric driver trees and decision-support performance reporting -> business-analytics-kpi.
Strategic options, market positioning, unit-economic business cases and operating-model choice -> business-strategy-analysis.
Detailed event-level SQL or cohort computation -> data/data-analytics-metrics.
