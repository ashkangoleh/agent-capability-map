# Purpose

Diagnose organic search problems using observable crawl, indexation, page, content, and search-performance evidence.

## Inputs and prerequisites

Establish site, market, language, target pages, business goal, CMS/stack, access to crawl data, Search Console, analytics, and relevant search-engine documentation.

## Procedure

1. Define the SEO objective and scope: technical health, discovery, indexation, relevance, authority, or conversion.
2. Inspect crawl data, robots directives, XML sitemaps, canonical tags, redirects, status codes, rendering, internal links, and page templates.
3. Separate crawlability from indexability and ranking. A crawlable URL is not necessarily indexed.
4. Map query intent and page intent. Identify cannibalization, thin or duplicate content, and missing coverage using actual page and query data.
5. Inspect structured data against current official specifications and test results. Do not promise rich-result display.
6. Review internal linking, site architecture, localization, and mobile performance where relevant.
7. Use Search Console and analytics to distinguish impressions, clicks, sessions, conversions, and attribution limitations.
8. Prioritize findings by evidence, impact hypothesis, confidence, effort, and dependencies.
9. Verify current search-engine policies from official sources when advice depends on current guidance.
10. Define a measurement and re-evaluation plan.

## Evidence requirements

Use crawl exports, rendered HTML, Search Console, analytics, page samples, and current official search documentation. Mark estimates and hypotheses.

## Output contract

Provide scope, evidence, findings, severity, affected URLs/templates, recommended fixes, validation method, and measurement plan.

## Failure modes and abstention

Never guarantee rankings, traffic growth, indexing, rich results, or inclusion in AI-generated answers. Do not label a tactic a ranking factor without reliable evidence.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Crawlability, indexability, relevance, authority, and conversion are distinguished.
- [ ] Findings point to affected URLs or templates.
- [ ] Recommendations have observable validation steps.
- [ ] Current policies are verified where relevant.
- [ ] No ranking or traffic guarantee is made.
