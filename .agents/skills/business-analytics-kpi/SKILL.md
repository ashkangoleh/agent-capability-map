# Business analytics and KPI decision support

## Activate
Use for management-facing KPI definitions, performance decomposition, business review scorecards, driver trees, or business metric tradeoffs. Use data-analytics-metrics for detailed SQL and cohort computations; business-strategy-analysis for strategy and business-case options.

## Procedure
1. Identify the business outcome, decision rights, leading/lagging indicators and reporting cadence.
2. Build a concise metric tree with definition, formula, population, denominator, source owner, freshness SLA and guardrails.
3. Trace dependencies between revenue, conversion, retention, costs, unit economics and service quality without claiming causal links absent evidence.
4. Verify consistency of period, geography, units, currency, cost allocation and cohort definitions.
5. Separate actuals, forecasts, scenarios and assumptions. Express sensitivity to data completeness or behavioral changes.
6. Recommend the smallest actionable scorecard and define what operational decision each metric informs.

## Outputs
KPI dictionary, traceable source map, scorecard specification, assumptions, potential failure modes and verification checks. Never invent company-specific performance or unsupported targets.
