# podcast-production-brief: specialist evaluation and negative controls

Domain `media/core`. This is a **proposed** evidence plan, never a real-system test result or execution permission.

## Specialist required inputs
Verify specialist prerequisites against real artifacts.

## Preserve skill-specific method
1. Write episode thesis, listener payoff, outline and research questions with claims requiring sources.
2. Confirm participants, releases, privacy and recording permissions, and distinguish host editorial opinion from verified fact.
3. Plan interview flow, backup questions and accessible speaking/description cues.
4. Prepare recording QA for room noise, clipping, mic consistency and backup files without inventing hardware settings.

## Challenge / acceptance
- **Challenge:** Replace a referenced clip or alter timecode and track licensing. Exercise an authorized contradictory, missing or adversarial test input.
- **Independent oracle:** Asset provenance, editorial rights and render quality are verified by sample review. Reconcile observed outcomes against a fixed control; do not use the generated answer as its own oracle.
- **Existing failure risks:** Unknown evidence remains UNKNOWN.
- **Stop/review:** No fabricated provenance, consent or rights claims. Stop at incomplete authorizations, uncertain authoritative sources and irreversible changes.

## Acceptance evidence ledger
- [ ] Real source artifacts, version/effective date, scope, owner, permissions and lower-trust input boundary recorded.
- [ ] At least one baseline, negative control, ambiguity/near miss and recovery/retraction test documented with falsifiable expected outcomes.
- [ ] Domain-specific result, affected population, uncertainty, metrics and policy constraints independently verified.
- [ ] For implementation: isolated executable compatibility/failure tests, side effects and rollback. For advisory work: qualified independent review and citation audit instead.
- [ ] Simpler alternative, operational cost, incident/escalation owner and explicitly unresolved uncertainties stated.
- [ ] Separate proposed test from executed evidence; never claim production, legal, medical, security or regulatory certification from CI.
