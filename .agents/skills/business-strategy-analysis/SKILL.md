# Purpose

Support business decisions with transparent assumptions, comparable options, quantified trade-offs, and explicit uncertainty.

## Inputs and prerequisites

Identify decision, time horizon, geography, stakeholders, baseline, constraints, available data, and reversibility.

## Procedure

1. Define the decision and what success means.
2. Separate known facts, management assumptions, estimates, and unknowns.
3. Map value drivers, cost drivers, constraints, dependencies, and externalities.
4. Build comparable options, including the status quo.
5. Quantify economics only from provided or sourced inputs. Show formulas, units, periods, and assumptions.
6. Run sensitivity or scenario analysis for material uncertain variables.
7. Assess execution complexity, operational risk, opportunity cost, and reversibility.
8. Recommend an option with conditions that would change the decision.

## Evidence requirements

Use actual operating data, contracts, market sources, and explicit assumptions. Never invent revenue, margin, market share, conversion, or financial outcomes.

## Output contract

Provide decision framing, evidence, assumptions, options, trade-offs, sensitivity, recommendation, risks, and decision triggers.

## Failure modes and abstention

Do not present a scenario as a forecast or provide regulated personal financial advice. If inputs are missing, show the model structure and identify required inputs rather than fabricating values.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Status quo is considered.
- [ ] Options use comparable criteria.
- [ ] Calculations expose formulas and units.
- [ ] Uncertainty and sensitivities are explicit.
- [ ] Recommendation includes decision triggers.
