# humanities-primary-source-critique: specialist evaluation and negative controls

Domain `humanities/core`. This is a **proposed** evidence plan, never a real-system test result or execution permission.

## Specialist required inputs
Verify specialist prerequisites against real artifacts.

## Preserve skill-specific method
1. Identify original artifacts and distinguish primary records from later interpretations.
2. Record provenance, date, author, archive or catalog ID, edition, transcription and uncertainty.
3. Contextualize language, audience, genre and historical power relations without assuming modern meanings.
4. Compare independent sources and document contradictions, missing records or potential biases.

## Challenge / acceptance
- **Challenge:** Provide contradictory editions, translations or chronology. Exercise an authorized contradictory, missing or adversarial test input.
- **Independent oracle:** Primary/secondary sources are distinguished and claims trace to their context. Reconcile observed outcomes against a fixed control; do not use the generated answer as its own oracle.
- **Existing failure risks:** Unknown evidence remains UNKNOWN.
- **Stop/review:** No invented quotations, archives, dates or consensus. Stop at incomplete authorizations, uncertain authoritative sources and irreversible changes.

## Acceptance evidence ledger
- [ ] Real source artifacts, version/effective date, scope, owner, permissions and lower-trust input boundary recorded.
- [ ] At least one baseline, negative control, ambiguity/near miss and recovery/retraction test documented with falsifiable expected outcomes.
- [ ] Domain-specific result, affected population, uncertainty, metrics and policy constraints independently verified.
- [ ] For implementation: isolated executable compatibility/failure tests, side effects and rollback. For advisory work: qualified independent review and citation audit instead.
- [ ] Simpler alternative, operational cost, incident/escalation owner and explicitly unresolved uncertainties stated.
- [ ] Separate proposed test from executed evidence; never claim production, legal, medical, security or regulatory certification from CI.
