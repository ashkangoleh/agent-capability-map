# Purpose

Build a measurable marketing plan tied to a business objective, audience, positioning, channel economics, and execution constraints.

## Inputs and prerequisites

Establish objective, product/offer, target geography, audience evidence, funnel stage, budget, timeline, channels, brand constraints, and available measurement.

## Procedure

1. Clarify the business outcome and the marketing outcome; do not treat impressions as revenue.
2. Define audience segments from evidence. Label personas as hypotheses if research is absent.
3. State positioning, value proposition, proof points, objections, and message hierarchy.
4. Map funnel stages and the role of each channel.
5. Recommend a focused channel mix with rationale, dependencies, and budget assumptions.
6. Define creative tests, landing-page requirements, conversion events, attribution limits, and guardrail metrics.
7. Set a measurement plan with baseline, target, owner, cadence, and decision thresholds.
8. Verify current platform formats, policy restrictions, targeting options, and pricing where relevant.
9. Include risks, privacy/consent requirements, and a learning agenda.

## Evidence requirements

Use actual customer research, analytics, campaign history, market data, and current official platform documentation. Never invent ROAS, CAC, CPM, audience size, competitor performance, or campaign outcomes.

## Output contract

Provide objective, audience, positioning, funnel, channel plan, budget assumptions, creative tests, measurement, risks, and next decisions.

## Failure modes and abstention

Do not forecast outcomes without explicit assumptions and uncertainty ranges. Do not guarantee reach, conversions, or revenue. Escalate regulated claims and sensitive targeting for compliance review.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Business and marketing objectives are distinct.
- [ ] Audience claims are evidence-backed or labeled hypotheses.
- [ ] Channels map to funnel roles.
- [ ] Budget and performance estimates expose assumptions.
- [ ] Measurement and decision rules are defined.
