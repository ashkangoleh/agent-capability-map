# marketing-creator-disclosure-review

## Activation boundary
Review influencer campaign disclosures and substantiation before sponsored publication. Do not assume provider features, law, account eligibility, real campaign performance, or tool permissions.

## Required inputs
Country and platform, sponsor/creator relationship, compensation, claim evidence, asset format, publication date and review workflow. Determine actual platform/API release, market/jurisdiction, consent and business decision owner before execution.

## Specialist procedure
1. Identify material financial or nonfinancial ties and applicable platform/jurisdiction disclosure requirements.
2. Require clear conspicuous disclosure positioned with the actual endorsement rather than hidden in metadata.
3. Verify claim substantiation and special restrictions for regulated categories; do not claim legal clearance from generic checklists.
4. Inspect example post, video and stories across devices/placements, archiving evidence and approval trail.
5. Define correction, takedown and escalation owners for missing disclosure or unsupported statements.

## Operational failure and recovery
- Failure: Paid relationship is omitted and a material endorsement becomes misleading.
- Containment: stop or quarantine the affected campaign, audience, conversion uploads or spend pathway as appropriate; identify the authorised incident owner.
- Recovery: fix the source issue, replay/reconcile against a frozen ledger or sample, verify that late events or previous recipients are not double-counted, then resume by approved staged release.
- Do not modify remote platforms, send messages, spend budget, export personal data, or claim account access without explicit authorization.

## Independent falsification
- Negative/adversarial case: Creator proposes a buried disclosure after many hashtags; reject before publication.
- Independent acceptance oracle: For sampled sponsored content, reviewers independently locate disclosure and verify every objective claim.
- Record actual observed evidence, test environment, sample coverage, expected outcome and uncertainty; a proposed test is not an executed test.

## Official source starting points
- https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers
Check the exact published/current version, effective date, API eligibility and local rules at execution; source URLs are not operational certification.

## Output contract
Produce a scoped artifact, decision criteria, evidence/source ledger, assumptions, failure thresholds, test cases, owner, rollback trigger and unresolved risks. Separate observed facts, hypotheses and recommendations. Do not fabricate audience sizes, conversion rates, ROAS or impact estimates.

## Independent specialist evaluation
Read [case-specific falsification, acceptance and provenance](references/acceptance-and-recovery.md) on demand for production-impacting work. Passing static registry checks does not prove correctness or authorize any action.
