# marketing-brand-message-validation

## Activation boundary
Validate positioning and advertising claims before publishing creative. Do not assume provider features, law, account eligibility, real campaign performance, or tool permissions.

## Required inputs
Product evidence, message variants, claims inventory, competitor source dates, legal constraints and target segment. Determine actual platform/API release, market/jurisdiction, consent and business decision owner before execution.

## Specialist procedure
1. Translate positioning into audience, differentiated benefit, explicit evidence and a constrained promise.
2. Audit objective product, savings, performance and environmental claims against primary source and applicable market rules.
3. Separate recall and preference testing from causal business outcomes; prevent post-hoc winning-message selection.
4. Use blinded concept tests where feasible, record recruitment bias, report objections and subgroup uncertainty.
5. Produce a message hierarchy with approved proof points, rejected claims and change triggers.

## Operational failure and recovery
- Failure: An unverified superiority or sustainability claim is promoted as a verified fact.
- Containment: stop or quarantine the affected campaign, audience, conversion uploads or spend pathway as appropriate; identify the authorised incident owner.
- Recovery: fix the source issue, replay/reconcile against a frozen ledger or sample, verify that late events or previous recipients are not double-counted, then resume by approved staged release.
- Do not modify remote platforms, send messages, spend budget, export personal data, or claim account access without explicit authorization.

## Independent falsification
- Negative/adversarial case: A competitor comparison cites outdated product terms; do not publish the comparative claim.
- Independent acceptance oracle: Every material message claim has a current source, approval status and reproducible supporting test.
- Record actual observed evidence, test environment, sample coverage, expected outcome and uncertainty; a proposed test is not an executed test.

## Official source starting points
- https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing
Check the exact published/current version, effective date, API eligibility and local rules at execution; source URLs are not operational certification.

## Output contract
Produce a scoped artifact, decision criteria, evidence/source ledger, assumptions, failure thresholds, test cases, owner, rollback trigger and unresolved risks. Separate observed facts, hypotheses and recommendations. Do not fabricate audience sizes, conversion rates, ROAS or impact estimates.

## Independent specialist evaluation
Read [case-specific falsification, acceptance and provenance](references/acceptance-and-recovery.md) on demand for production-impacting work. Passing static registry checks does not prove correctness or authorize any action.
