# public-policy-program-evaluation: specialist evaluation and negative controls

Domain `public-sector/core`. This is a **proposed** evidence plan, never a real-system test result or execution permission.

## Specialist required inputs
Verify specialist prerequisites against real artifacts.

## Preserve skill-specific method
1. Define policy theory of change, inputs, activities, outputs and actual outcomes separately.
2. Identify stakeholders, access and equity implications, spillovers and measurement harms.
3. Use OECD criteria (relevance, coherence, effectiveness, efficiency, impact and sustainability) selectively, not as a mechanical checklist.
4. Specify causal identification approach when impact is claimed; explicitly document the counterfactual and confounding limits.

## Challenge / acceptance
- **Challenge:** Change governing rule, jurisdiction or impacted population. Exercise an authorized contradictory, missing or adversarial test input.
- **Independent oracle:** Decision has current legal basis, traceable evidence and equitable impact assessment. Reconcile observed outcomes against a fixed control; do not use the generated answer as its own oracle.
- **Existing failure risks:** Unknown evidence remains UNKNOWN.
- **Stop/review:** No binding public decision, eligibility approval or policy compliance claim. Stop at incomplete authorizations, uncertain authoritative sources and irreversible changes.

## Acceptance evidence ledger
- [ ] Real source artifacts, version/effective date, scope, owner, permissions and lower-trust input boundary recorded.
- [ ] At least one baseline, negative control, ambiguity/near miss and recovery/retraction test documented with falsifiable expected outcomes.
- [ ] Domain-specific result, affected population, uncertainty, metrics and policy constraints independently verified.
- [ ] For implementation: isolated executable compatibility/failure tests, side effects and rollback. For advisory work: qualified independent review and citation audit instead.
- [ ] Simpler alternative, operational cost, incident/escalation owner and explicitly unresolved uncertainties stated.
- [ ] Separate proposed test from executed evidence; never claim production, legal, medical, security or regulatory certification from CI.
