# travel-itinerary-source-review: specialist evaluation and negative controls

Domain `travel/core`. This is a **proposed** evidence plan, never a real-system test result or execution permission.

## Specialist required inputs
Verify specialist prerequisites against real artifacts.

## Preserve skill-specific method
1. Separate immutable destination facts from volatile opening hours, transport availability, entry rules and security advisories.
2. Consult official destination advisories and transport/operator sites; record source dates and traveler-specific applicability.
3. Group feasible activities by geography and scheduling constraints; avoid inventing routes, time estimates or availability.
4. Check accessibility, closure dates, seasonal weather risk and reservation dependencies where material.

## Challenge / acceptance
- **Challenge:** Cancel one connection or change local visa/transport requirement. Exercise an authorized contradictory, missing or adversarial test input.
- **Independent oracle:** Itinerary feasibility and source freshness clearly stated with alternatives. Reconcile observed outcomes against a fixed control; do not use the generated answer as its own oracle.
- **Existing failure risks:** Unknown evidence remains UNKNOWN.
- **Stop/review:** No guaranteed fare, availability, entry permission or safe travel condition. Stop at incomplete authorizations, uncertain authoritative sources and irreversible changes.

## Acceptance evidence ledger
- [ ] Real source artifacts, version/effective date, scope, owner, permissions and lower-trust input boundary recorded.
- [ ] At least one baseline, negative control, ambiguity/near miss and recovery/retraction test documented with falsifiable expected outcomes.
- [ ] Domain-specific result, affected population, uncertainty, metrics and policy constraints independently verified.
- [ ] For implementation: isolated executable compatibility/failure tests, side effects and rollback. For advisory work: qualified independent review and citation audit instead.
- [ ] Simpler alternative, operational cost, incident/escalation owner and explicitly unresolved uncertainties stated.
- [ ] Separate proposed test from executed evidence; never claim production, legal, medical, security or regulatory certification from CI.
