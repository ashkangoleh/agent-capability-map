# research-handoff-reproducible-findings: scoped specialist falsification and recovery

Route `research/knowledge-transfer/handoff-reproducible-findings`. L1 structural plan, no independently validated live research or agent output.

## Research domain profile
- Challenge: Alter sampling assumptions or introduce contradictory primary evidence
- Independent oracle: Methods, uncertainty, independent replication and provenance are disclosed
- Stop policy: No fabricated citations, research result or significance claim

## Critical case
- Failure: Handoff claims experiments passed but only a README and screenshots exist.
- Independent acceptance: Recipient can reproduce verified steps and clearly identify unverified work.
- Recovery: quarantine conclusion, record affected sources and audience, obtain reviewer approval, correct/retract if already distributed and preserve evidence trail.

## Acceptance evidence ledger
- [ ] Freeze source artifact/version/date, query and exact decision scope; identify owner and permissions.
- [ ] Positive, negative, near-miss, ambiguous and missing-source fixtures have documented expected outcomes.
- [ ] Trace all material claims and distinguish expert review from a generated plausibility summary.
- [ ] Check conflicting evidence, attribution, rights, sensitive data access, implementation or analysis version compatibility.
- [ ] Document the simplest alternative, correction and rollback route, with actual observed evaluations separately from proposed tests.
- [ ] Never treat this document as proof of external fact-check success, compliance or real-agent host readiness.
