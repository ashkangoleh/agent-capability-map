# business-commercial-contract-obligations: independent acceptance and recovery

Route `business/partnerships-contracts/commercial-contract-obligations`. Structural contract only; no execution, certification, observed return or expert signoff.

## Portfolio policy
- Challenge: Alter revenue, cost or customer cohort assumptions and recompute the decision
- Independent oracle: Unit economics, downside risk and provenance survive sensitivity analysis
- Stop: No guaranteed ROI, market result or invented business benchmark

## Domain-specific falsification
- Failure: Auto renewal occurs because notice window and owner were never recorded.
- Independent target: Verified term-to-action register maps each obligation to source clause.
- Rollback or remediation: hold approval or supplier/change action, preserve input version and owner, reconcile source evidence and rerun bounded tests before release.

## Acceptance evidence ledger
- [ ] Source and baseline, timestamp, jurisdiction, business owner, permissions and units documented.
- [ ] Positive, negative, near-miss, contradictory and missing-evidence inputs checked independently.
- [ ] Reconcile cash versus accrual, sample totals, contract ownership, process exceptions or supplier metrics where applicable.
- [ ] Verify adoption/cost or risk impact against a frozen evidence ledger; separate observed facts from scenarios.
- [ ] Review cheaper or simpler alternative, blast radius, approved stop gate and escalation owner.
- [ ] No claim of real business certification, audited financials, legal binding outcome or causal improvement from this plan.
