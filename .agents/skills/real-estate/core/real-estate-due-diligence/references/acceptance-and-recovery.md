# real-estate-due-diligence: specialist evaluation and negative controls

Domain `real-estate/core`. This is a **proposed** evidence plan, never a real-system test result or execution permission.

## Specialist required inputs
Verify specialist prerequisites against real artifacts.

## Preserve skill-specific method
1. Define objective and property interest; separate market comparison from formal certified valuation.
2. Inspect verified title/tenure, easements, property conditions and planning restrictions through authorized local sources.
3. Normalize comparable units, dates, area definitions, currency and condition adjustments transparently.
4. Evaluate operating expenses, vacancy, maintenance, taxes and financing with scenario uncertainty.

## Challenge / acceptance
- **Challenge:** Change property condition, zoning or comparable timeframe. Exercise an authorized contradictory, missing or adversarial test input.
- **Independent oracle:** Price assumptions and compliance uncertainties are explicit and traceable. Reconcile observed outcomes against a fixed control; do not use the generated answer as its own oracle.
- **Existing failure risks:** Unknown evidence remains UNKNOWN.
- **Stop/review:** No property valuation or regulatory guarantee from unsupported listing data. Stop at incomplete authorizations, uncertain authoritative sources and irreversible changes.

## Acceptance evidence ledger
- [ ] Real source artifacts, version/effective date, scope, owner, permissions and lower-trust input boundary recorded.
- [ ] At least one baseline, negative control, ambiguity/near miss and recovery/retraction test documented with falsifiable expected outcomes.
- [ ] Domain-specific result, affected population, uncertainty, metrics and policy constraints independently verified.
- [ ] For implementation: isolated executable compatibility/failure tests, side effects and rollback. For advisory work: qualified independent review and citation audit instead.
- [ ] Simpler alternative, operational cost, incident/escalation owner and explicitly unresolved uncertainties stated.
- [ ] Separate proposed test from executed evidence; never claim production, legal, medical, security or regulatory certification from CI.
