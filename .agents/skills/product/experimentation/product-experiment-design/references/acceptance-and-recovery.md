# product-experiment-design: specialist evaluation and negative controls

Domain `product/experimentation`. This is a **proposed** evidence plan, never a real-system test result or execution permission.

## Specialist required inputs
Verify specialist prerequisites against real artifacts.

## Preserve skill-specific method
1. Turn the product change into a falsifiable hypothesis and determine the unit of randomization and analysis; consider users, devices, households and network spillovers.
2. Define primary outcome and guardrails with precise exposure, numerator, denominator, population, time windows and novelty/seasonality considerations.
3. Specify assignment persistency, concurrent experiment interference, sample-ratio mismatch, missing events, bots and identity changes before discussing statistical power.
4. Pre-register the analysis plan: hypothesis, minimum detectable effect, sample sizing method, stopping rule, multiplicity and handling of outliers/missing values.

## Challenge / acceptance
- **Challenge:** Introduce a conflicting stakeholder request or change activation metric definition. Exercise an authorized contradictory, missing or adversarial test input.
- **Independent oracle:** Research-backed prioritization and falsifiable release metric survive the change. Reconcile observed outcomes against a fixed control; do not use the generated answer as its own oracle.
- **Existing failure risks:** Unknown evidence remains UNKNOWN.
- **Stop/review:** No guaranteed adoption or unverified business uplift. Stop at incomplete authorizations, uncertain authoritative sources and irreversible changes.

## Acceptance evidence ledger
- [ ] Real source artifacts, version/effective date, scope, owner, permissions and lower-trust input boundary recorded.
- [ ] At least one baseline, negative control, ambiguity/near miss and recovery/retraction test documented with falsifiable expected outcomes.
- [ ] Domain-specific result, affected population, uncertainty, metrics and policy constraints independently verified.
- [ ] For implementation: isolated executable compatibility/failure tests, side effects and rollback. For advisory work: qualified independent review and citation audit instead.
- [ ] Simpler alternative, operational cost, incident/escalation owner and explicitly unresolved uncertainties stated.
- [ ] Separate proposed test from executed evidence; never claim production, legal, medical, security or regulatory certification from CI.
