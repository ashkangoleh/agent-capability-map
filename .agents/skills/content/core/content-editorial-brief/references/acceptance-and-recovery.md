# content-editorial-brief: specialist evaluation and negative controls

Domain `content/core`. This is a **proposed** evidence plan, never a real-system test result or execution permission.

## Specialist required inputs
Verify specialist prerequisites against real artifacts.

## Preserve skill-specific method
1. Define reader problem, audience stage, desired action and what useful original information or first-hand expertise is available. Avoid drafting generic search-only filler.
2. Specify scope, angle, key questions, claims requiring verification, outline and source ownership; mark research gaps rather than inventing quotes or statistics.
3. Separate editorial value from SEO optimization; keywords and headings should support comprehension, not force length or keyword stuffing.
4. Respect copyright and attribution; never paraphrase an uninspected article as though it were read or fabricate interviews, credentials or citations.

## Challenge / acceptance
- **Challenge:** Introduce an unsupported claim or unattributed source in draft content. Exercise an authorized contradictory, missing or adversarial test input.
- **Independent oracle:** Claims are fact-checked, rights documented and content meets the target audience purpose. Reconcile observed outcomes against a fixed control; do not use the generated answer as its own oracle.
- **Existing failure risks:** Unknown evidence remains UNKNOWN.
- **Stop/review:** No fabricated citations, editorial approvals or originality guarantees. Stop at incomplete authorizations, uncertain authoritative sources and irreversible changes.

## Acceptance evidence ledger
- [ ] Real source artifacts, version/effective date, scope, owner, permissions and lower-trust input boundary recorded.
- [ ] At least one baseline, negative control, ambiguity/near miss and recovery/retraction test documented with falsifiable expected outcomes.
- [ ] Domain-specific result, affected population, uncertainty, metrics and policy constraints independently verified.
- [ ] For implementation: isolated executable compatibility/failure tests, side effects and rollback. For advisory work: qualified independent review and citation audit instead.
- [ ] Simpler alternative, operational cost, incident/escalation owner and explicitly unresolved uncertainties stated.
- [ ] Separate proposed test from executed evidence; never claim production, legal, medical, security or regulatory certification from CI.
