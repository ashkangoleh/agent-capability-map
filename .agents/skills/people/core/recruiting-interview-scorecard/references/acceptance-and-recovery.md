# recruiting-interview-scorecard: specialist evaluation and negative controls

Domain `people/core`. This is a **proposed** evidence plan, never a real-system test result or execution permission.

## Specialist required inputs
Verify specialist prerequisites against real artifacts.

## Preserve skill-specific method
1. Translate actual job duties into observable competencies; avoid irrelevant personality proxies or protected-characteristic inferences.
2. Create consistent behavioral or work-sample questions per competency, with job-related success and failure examples.
3. Use anchored rating levels, evidence notes and independent interviewer scoring before group discussion.
4. Provide candidate accommodations and privacy safeguards, retention limits and accessible formats in line with verified local rules.

## Challenge / acceptance
- **Challenge:** Change eligibility policy or expose sensitive applicant details. Exercise an authorized contradictory, missing or adversarial test input.
- **Independent oracle:** Evaluation rationale, lawful processing and appeals are auditable and proportionate. Reconcile observed outcomes against a fixed control; do not use the generated answer as its own oracle.
- **Existing failure risks:** Unknown evidence remains UNKNOWN.
- **Stop/review:** No employment law compliance or discriminatory recommendation without review. Stop at incomplete authorizations, uncertain authoritative sources and irreversible changes.

## Acceptance evidence ledger
- [ ] Real source artifacts, version/effective date, scope, owner, permissions and lower-trust input boundary recorded.
- [ ] At least one baseline, negative control, ambiguity/near miss and recovery/retraction test documented with falsifiable expected outcomes.
- [ ] Domain-specific result, affected population, uncertainty, metrics and policy constraints independently verified.
- [ ] For implementation: isolated executable compatibility/failure tests, side effects and rollback. For advisory work: qualified independent review and citation audit instead.
- [ ] Simpler alternative, operational cost, incident/escalation owner and explicitly unresolved uncertainties stated.
- [ ] Separate proposed test from executed evidence; never claim production, legal, medical, security or regulatory certification from CI.
