# carbon-inventory-boundary-review: specialist evaluation and negative controls

Domain `energy/core`. This is a **proposed** evidence plan, never a real-system test result or execution permission.

## Specialist required inputs
Verify specialist prerequisites against real artifacts.

## Preserve skill-specific method
1. Confirm organizational and operational boundaries and choose the applicable standard/reporting vintage.
2. Classify sources by direct, purchased energy and relevant value-chain categories; avoid double counting across facilities.
3. Record activity data, units, conversion factors, factor date, geography and supporting evidence.
4. Calculate emissions with explicit formula and GWP basis; preserve uncertainty and missing-data estimates.

## Challenge / acceptance
- **Challenge:** Alter demand, generation availability or a safety limit. Exercise an authorized contradictory, missing or adversarial test input.
- **Independent oracle:** Conservation/accounting, operating envelope and fault handling are checked against applicable technical requirements. Reconcile observed outcomes against a fixed control; do not use the generated answer as its own oracle.
- **Existing failure risks:** Unknown evidence remains UNKNOWN.
- **Stop/review:** No live grid control or safety-critical operational advice without authorized engineer. Stop at incomplete authorizations, uncertain authoritative sources and irreversible changes.

## Acceptance evidence ledger
- [ ] Real source artifacts, version/effective date, scope, owner, permissions and lower-trust input boundary recorded.
- [ ] At least one baseline, negative control, ambiguity/near miss and recovery/retraction test documented with falsifiable expected outcomes.
- [ ] Domain-specific result, affected population, uncertainty, metrics and policy constraints independently verified.
- [ ] For implementation: isolated executable compatibility/failure tests, side effects and rollback. For advisory work: qualified independent review and citation audit instead.
- [ ] Simpler alternative, operational cost, incident/escalation owner and explicitly unresolved uncertainties stated.
- [ ] Separate proposed test from executed evidence; never claim production, legal, medical, security or regulatory certification from CI.
